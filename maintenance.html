Sheet 02 · Maintenance

Maintenance visits.

A planned visit to work through the small stuff before it becomes the expensive stuff. For homes, rental properties and small business premises.

Book a maintenance visit Build your list

01What a visit covers

Inside, outside, and your own list.

Every visit is planned around the property and what’s on your mind. These are the things we look at most often.

Inside

A

The wear points that fail quietly until they don’t.

  • Faucets, toilets and drains checked for drips, runs and slow drains
  • Caulk and grout around tubs, showers and sinks
  • Doors, latches, hinges and locks
  • Smoke and CO alarms tested, batteries swapped
  • Cabinet hinges, drawer slides and loose hardware
  • Wall dings, nail pops and paint touch-ups

Outside

B

The jobs weather makes worse every season they’re left.

  • Gutters cleared, downspouts checked
  • Exterior caulking at windows and doors
  • Weatherstripping and door sweeps
  • Fence boards, gates and latches
  • Deck boards and railings
  • Outdoor faucets opened up or shut down for the season

Your list

C

Anything else you’ve been saving up. Send it with your booking and we’ll plan the visit around it, bringing the parts we can predict.

  • Small repairs from the repairs list
  • Small improvements, like a shelf or a new fixture
  • A second opinion on something that’s bothering you

02After the visit

You get a written summary.

What we did, what we noticed, and what’s worth planning for next. With photos, so you can see it without climbing a ladder.

Useful for keeping track of your own home, and handy for landlords who need a record of what was done in each unit and when.

04For landlords

Turnovers, and the calls in between.

Between tenants the clock is running. During a tenancy, you need repairs handled without being the go-between for every message.

Turnover touch-upsBetween tenants
  • Patch and paint: nail holes, scuffs, picture-hook damage
  • Doors, locks and handles working and tight
  • Caulk and grout refreshed in kitchens and bathrooms
  • Blinds, screens, towel bars and fixtures replaced or repaired
  • Smoke and CO alarms tested and dated
  • Photos of every completed item for your file

How it works with rentals

We can work from your move-out notes, or walk the unit with you first and write the list together.

For repairs during a tenancy, send us the tenant’s contact details and we’ll arrange access with them directly, keeping you copied on every step.

Each job gets a short written summary with photos, so you have a record of what was done and when.

Book a turnover visit

05For business premises

Keep the doors open. Literally.

Shops, studios, offices and clinics see more wear than most homes: more doors opened, more fixtures knocked, more scuffs per square foot.

Regular visits keep the small stuff from piling up: door closers adjusted, hardware tightened, walls patched, fixtures and shelving secured. We plan visits around your opening hours so customers don’t notice we were there, except that things work.
