Sheet 04 · Process

How it works.

What happens after you get in touch, step by step, and who does what at each one. Plus straight answers to the questions people ask most.

Request a repair Jump to the FAQ

01The process

Five steps. No mystery.

The same order whether it’s a single faucet or a full maintenance visit.

  1. 01 / 05

    You tell us what needs fixing.

    You

    Send the request form or email us. Add photos if you can, and anything you already know: when it started, what you’ve tried, the make or model.

    MK

    Read it properly and work out what else we need to know. One clear question now saves a wasted trip later.

  2. 02 / 05

    We reply with questions or a plan.

    You

    Answer anything we ask. Sometimes that’s a closer photo; sometimes it’s a model number or a measurement.

    MK

    Reply by email. Some jobs can be priced from photos. Others need a short look in person first. We’ll tell you which, and why.

  3. 03 / 05

    We agree the scope, the price and a time.

    You

    Say yes, ask questions, or change the list. Nothing is booked until you’re happy with it.

    MK

    Confirm in writing what we’ll do, what it costs and when we’ll arrive, so there’s nothing to remember or argue about later.

  4. 04 / 05

    We do the work.

    You

    Be there, or arrange access. If you’re a landlord, we can arrange it with your tenant.

    MK

    Protect floors and furniture, then get on with the job. If we find something unexpected, we stop and explain before going any further.

  5. 05 / 05

    We tidy up and show you.

    You

    Look it over. If something isn’t right, tell us. That’s what the walkthrough is for.

    MK

    Clean up, walk you through what was done, and mention anything else we noticed. If you weren’t home, you get photos and a short written summary.

02Until we get there

Keep it from getting worse.

Some problems can sit for a week. Some shouldn’t be left alone overnight. Here’s how to hold the line between your request and our visit. Not fixed, just not worse.

CodeProblemUntil we get there
  • PLB

    Leak under a sinkDo this now

    Turn the shutoff valves under the sink clockwise until they stop, put a bowl underneath, and empty the cabinet so it can dry out.

  • PLB

    Toilet that won’t stop runningDo this now

    Turn the valve on the wall behind the toilet clockwise to stop the water. Turn it back on when you need to flush.

  • PLB

    Water coming through a ceilingDo this now

    Put a bucket under it, move furniture and electronics out of the way, and shut off the water to whatever is above. If the ceiling is sagging, stay out of the room. Don’t touch lights or outlets near the water.

  • FLR

    Cracked tile or grout in a showerDo this now

    Use another shower if you have one. Water getting behind tile does more damage than the crack itself.

  • CRP

    Loose handrailDo this now

    Don’t lean on it, and put a strip of tape or a note on it so visitors don’t either.

  • EXT

    Gutter overflowing or pulled looseDo this now

    Stay off the ladder. Move anything sitting where the water is landing, and leave the rest to us.

  • DOR

    Door that sticks or won’t latchCan wait

    Close it gently rather than forcing it. If it’s an outside door that won’t lock, mention that in your request.

  • WAL

    Hole in the drywallCan wait

    Nothing. It’s waited this long; it can wait a few more days.

Smell gas? Leave the building, then call 911 or your gas utility from outside. That’s not a job for us, or for waiting.
