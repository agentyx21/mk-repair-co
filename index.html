02What you can expect

How a visit runs.

Small jobs deserve a proper job. This is how we work in your place, whether it’s a ten-minute fix or a full day on the list.

  1. 01

    We explain it first.

    What’s wrong, what the fix involves and what it costs. You agree to it before any work begins.

  2. 02

    We protect the space.

    Drop cloths down, furniture moved with care, dust kept where it belongs.

  3. 03

    We fix the cause, not just the symptom.

    If the “small” job turns out to be a bigger one, we stop and talk it through with you before going further.

  4. 04

    We clean up.

    The room goes back the way we found it, minus the problem. Old parts and packaging leave with us.

  5. 05

    We show you what we did.

    A quick walkthrough, or photos if you weren’t home. Plus anything we noticed that’s worth keeping an eye on.

03How it works

From “it’s broken” to “it’s fixed.”

No guesswork about what happens after you get in touch. Four steps, and you know where things stand at each one.

  1. 01

    Tell us what needs fixing

    Use the request form or email us. Photos help a lot.

  2. 02

    We reply with questions or a plan

    Some jobs can be priced from photos. Others need a quick look first. We’ll tell you which.

  3. 03

    We agree the work and a time

    Scope, price and arrival time, in writing, before anything starts.

  4. 04

    We fix it and tidy up

    Then we walk you through what was done, or send photos.

04Three kinds of list

Homes, rentals and the places you work.

Same standards, slightly different lists. Here’s what each tends to need from us.

Homeowners

The list that’s been on the fridge since spring, worked through in one visit where we can.

  • Sticking doors and drafty windows
  • Drywall patches and paint touch-ups
  • New fixtures, shelving and hardware

Landlords & property managers

Repairs during a tenancy and touch-ups between tenants, with notes and photos for your records.

  • Tenant-reported repairs
  • Turnover touch-ups before a unit is shown
  • Access arranged with tenants if you prefer

Small businesses

Shops, studios, offices and clinics that need the premises working, planned around your opening hours.

  • Doors, closers, locks and hardware
  • Shelving, fixtures and mounting
  • Wall repairs and paint between busy seasons

05Maintenance visits

A boring visit, on purpose.

A planned visit to work through the small stuff while it’s still small: caulk, gutters, hardware, alarms, drafts. Book one a season, once a year, or between tenants.
